HOW IT WORKS

Gentle interactions, lock-screen rituals, and safety nets.

🔒 The Lock Screen Overlay
DAWAII gently awakens over your lock screen 15 minutes after a scheduled dose if not yet recorded. It opens in a calm, dimmed state with an optional gentle chime to draw your attention without loud alarms.
👆 Single Tap · Wake & Focus
A single gentle tap anywhere on the screen brings the ambient display to full clarity and activates the postponement scrollwheel.
↔️ Scroll & Confirm · Postpone
Slide the horizontal wheel to select a delay (from 5 to 60 minutes). Tap the minimal Confirm button to reschedule your reminder.
✌️ Double Tap · Unlock / Dismiss
Double-tapping instantly dismisses the ambient overlay, returning you straight to your phone's normal system lock screen.
✨ Triple Tap · Mark Taken
Triple-tapping acknowledges your dose as taken immediately in your daily log, clearing all pending reminder sequences.
🌙 Night Shift Smart Window
Between 12:00 AM and 2:00 AM, if you turn your screen off to rest, DAWAII sounds two soft chimes. If the device remains asleep for 1 hour, it peacefully cancels the notification so your sleep is never disturbed.
📱 Caregiver SMS Sync
When enabled, if an evening window expires or a scheduled dose remains unacknowledged, DAWAII sends an automated SMS alert to your designated contact.
